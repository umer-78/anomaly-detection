02 How each detector scores

detectorideawhere it fails
randomuniform noiseeverywhere — it is the control
z-scoredistance from the mean in σmasking: mean and σ are dragged by the outliers
robust z-scoredistance from the median, scaled by MADstill blind to seasonality

ewmadistance from a moving expectationfollows a drift, so it will not report one
seasonal residualsubtract the daily shape, then judgeneeds to know the period

The threshold is the chosen quantile of the scores the detector produced on the series — never chosen from the labels. Strict F1 is judged point by point; point-adjusted F1 credits a whole segment from one hit, which is why the random control can look respectable on the adjusted number alone.
